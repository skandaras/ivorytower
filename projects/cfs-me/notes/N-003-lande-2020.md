# HLA associations in ME/CFS

## Summary
The study compared high-resolution HLA genotypes from 426 Norwegian adults with ME/CFS against 4,511 healthy, ethnically matched controls. It reports two allele associations, HLA-C*07:04 and HLA-DQB1*03:03, and a combined-carrier association. The abstract does not report allele-specific p-values or independent replication, so these findings should be treated as single-cohort associations in the information available here.
