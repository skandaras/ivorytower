# Familial clustering of CFS

## Summary
This study used linked genealogy and health-system diagnosis records in Utah to assess whether CFS clustered among relatives. Its abstract reports greater relatedness among cases and elevated relative risk among first-degree relatives. The accessible abstract text does not provide the study's sample size or complete relative-risk results, so the first-degree estimate is the only numerical relative-risk result included here.
