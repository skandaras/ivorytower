# Viral infections as possible ME/CFS triggers

## Summary
The EUROMENE review says ME/CFS has no established causative factors and that chronic viral infection as a cause has long been debated. It surveys herpesviruses (including EBV, CMV, HSV, VZV, HHV-6, HHV-7 and HHV-8), parvovirus B19, enteroviruses, retroviruses including XMRV, and Ross River virus, as well as discussing bacterial pathogens such as Mycoplasma, Borrelia and Coxiella in its framing of possible infectious triggers. Findings are mixed: some studies report associations or active infection in subgroups, but others find no difference or fail to replicate them. The authors conclude that infections could contribute in a subgroup, while no single infection is confirmed as a cause; a viral “hit and run” trigger remains a possibility, not a demonstrated explanation.

## Relevance to the project
This source directly examines viral candidates and rates their evidence as contested, heterogeneous and subgroup-limited. It does not establish any single pathogen as the certain cause, and explicitly says causative factors are not established.
