# Aetiology umbrella review

## Summary
This systematic umbrella review maps aetiological views across adult CFS/ME, fibromyalgia, irritable bowel syndrome, and somatic symptom/somatoform diagnoses. It includes narrative and systematic reviews with and without meta-analysis, searched through January 2022, and counts which domains and factors the reviews cover. The result is broad across syndromes and aetiological domains, but it does not pool primary CFS/ME predictor studies into one quantitative causal estimate: only a small fraction of the higher-quality systematic reviews were CFS/ME-specific, and the authors identify a lack of comprehensive biopsychosocial review models.

## Relevance to the project
This is a broad existing consolidation of review-level aetiology literature and directly weakens any categorical claim that no macro-level review exists. It is not, however, a complete ME/CFS-only quantitative trawl of primary predictor studies: its unit is the review, it includes other syndromes, and it reports that only two high-quality reviews were ME/CFS-specific. The narrow premise that no full-scope CFS/ME risk-factor synthesis has been established therefore survives this source; it does not justify treating any particular cause as known.

## Follow-ups
A current project-wide search could check whether a CFS/ME-only review after the January 2022 search date has subsequently filled this gap.
