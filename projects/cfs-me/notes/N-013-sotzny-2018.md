# Autoimmune model and candidate triggers

## Summary
This review argues that autoimmune mechanisms are supported in at least a subset of ME/CFS patients, while saying the underlying pathomechanism remains incompletely understood and may differ across subgroups. It describes infections, especially EBV, HHV-6 and parvovirus B19, as possible triggers of illness or autoimmunity. The authors summarize immune-cell changes, autoantibodies, genetic variants, metabolic disturbances and treatment findings as evidence consistent with autoimmunity, while documenting inconsistency in particular findings and the need for further work. Its level of claim is strong evidence for an autoimmune role in a subset, not certainty that ME/CFS as a whole is an autoimmune disease or that any one infection is causal.

## Relevance to the project
This review makes the strongest autoimmune claim among the requested sources, but limits it to a subset and presents candidate triggers and mechanisms without a universal certain cause. Autoimmunity is therefore a proposed subgroup mechanism, not a field-wide known cause under this review's own qualification.
