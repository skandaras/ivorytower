## Summary
The authors analyzed UK Biobank ME/CFS case-control cohorts using GWAS and combinatorial analysis. The abstract reports a set of SNPs and higher-order combinations associated with many cases, while also describing replication and comparison analyses using other UK Biobank-derived groups.
