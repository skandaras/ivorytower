# ME/CFS genetic association study

## Summary
This study tested ME/CFS cohorts from Norway, Denmark and UK Biobank. The abstract reports no genome-wide significant ME/CFS risk locus across the genome-wide analyses. A signal near TPPP was strongest in the Norwegian discovery cohort, but its top SNP did not replicate in the Danish cohort.
