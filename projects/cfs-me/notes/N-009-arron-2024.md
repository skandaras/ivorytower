# ME/CFS causes and biology review

## Summary
This 2024 review presents a broad account of ME/CFS causes, triggers, and pathophysiology. It discusses genetic susceptibility and proposed environmental triggers, including infections, emotional stress and major life events, toxins, vaccination, and drug exposure, then links these to immune, inflammatory, gut, vascular, and metabolic processes. It is a broad narrative synthesis, not a reported systematic quantitative trawl: the review description read here does not give an inclusion period or systematic study-design accounting, and the text presents uncertainty and conflicting findings in several areas.

## Relevance to the project
This source is newer and covers multiple relevant cause categories, so the idea that no broad cause-focused account exists under any name does not hold. It does not establish that a full-scope quantitative trawl has been done: the review presents an integrated account of proposed factors and mechanisms, but does not report systematic quantitative extraction across categories or primary-study designs. The brief's narrow quantitative premise survives these sources, though the claim should acknowledge existing broad narrative and umbrella reviews.

## Follow-ups
A systematic quantitative review could test whether proposed factor categories show replicated associations across consistent ME/CFS case definitions.
