# CFS/ME prevalence review

## Summary
This systematic review and meta-analysis consolidates population prevalence estimates, not evidence about causes. It combines 46 studies published from 1990 through 2018, and compares estimates across participant groups, case definitions, diagnostic methods, and countries. The authors exclude mechanistic, psychological, and treatment research, so it is a useful demographic/epidemiological consolidation but not a full-scope risk-factor trawl.

## Relevance to the project
This is a substantial quantitative demographic consolidation, but it does not test causes or triggers. Its exclusion of biological and psychological research means it does not invalidate the brief's specific premise about an all-domain causal trawl.

## Follow-ups
