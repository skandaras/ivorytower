# Infection and proposed mechanisms in Komaroff & Bateman

## Summary
This article focuses on post-infectious fatigue and the possibility that COVID-19 could lead to ME/CFS. It lists numerous viral, bacterial and parasitic infections followed by similar chronic fatigue syndromes, and notes that many, but not all, ME/CFS cases start with an infectious-like illness. The authors say the causes of post-infectious fatigue are uncertain, propose chronic low-grade neuroinflammation as one likely explanation for some lingering post-COVID fatigue, and discuss organ damage, psychiatric conditions, impaired energy production, oxidative stress, ion channelopathies and impaired cerebral perfusion as possible explanations or mechanisms. The article does not claim that SARS-CoV-2 or any single other cause is established as the cause of ME/CFS.
