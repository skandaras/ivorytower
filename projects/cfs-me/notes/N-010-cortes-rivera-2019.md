# Candidate causes in Cortes Rivera et al.

## Summary
The review describes ME/CFS as having unknown aetiology and unclear pathophysiological mechanisms. It treats infectious illness, especially viral infection, as a major possible initiating factor, while also discussing genetic predisposition, stress, immune dysfunction, inflammation and oxidative/nitrosative stress, autoimmunity, neuroendocrine disturbance, and metabolic or mitochondrial changes. It proposes interactions among nervous, immune and neuroendocrine systems rather than a single established cause. Evidence across the mechanisms is presented as variable and inconclusive, and the authors explicitly say that the origin of metabolic imbalance and the cause of infectious findings are not established.
