# Genetic risk review

## Summary
This critical review consolidates evidence on genetic contributions to ME/CFS risk, including family relatedness and heritability, mitochondrial and HLA genetics, candidate-gene studies, and genome-wide association studies. The authors find that candidate-gene results generally fail replication and that UK Biobank GWAS analyses do not converge on reproducible associations. Its scope is a single factor domain, so it is a genetic evidence review rather than a full-scope demographic, genetic, and environmental synthesis.

## Relevance to the project
This is a substantial synthesis for the genetic component of the brief's question, but it cannot establish whether a full-scope cross-domain trawl exists. Its conclusion also argues against treating a specific genetic association as a validated cause on the evidence reviewed.

## Follow-ups
