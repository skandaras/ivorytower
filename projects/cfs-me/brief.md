# Causes of myalgic encephalomyelitis

## Question
Are there any common demographic, genetic, or environmental factors at play for people who suffer from myalgic encephalomyelitis?

## Why this might be new
It's possible that a macro level, quantitative trawl has not been conducted across the full scope of existing research because of how relatively new the topic is but how many papers exist in the area.

## Scope
In: cause analysis, demographic research, trends in occurrences, novel treatment approaches
Out: anything needing lab work or simulation.

## Done when
A `known` verdict on a specific cause or trigger.

## Kill criteria
The literature already describes likely causes with certainty or validation of causes requires clinical effort.

## Starting points
Bateman and Bested and Bonilla, *Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome: Essentials of Diagnosis and Management* (2021)

## Notes for the planner
Gather widely, this will be a quantitative picture to paint that means lots of data consolidated. Probably building a database.
